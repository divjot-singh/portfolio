# Job Search Tracker

## Objective
Land a strong remote role in 4–5 months and keep relocation options open within 1 year.

## Strategy
### Track A: Remote-first roles
Target companies that hire globally or across time zones, especially:
- Europe-based remote companies
- Remote-first SaaS and AI tooling companies
- Developer platforms
- B2B product companies
- Companies with India-friendly hiring

### Track B: Relocation-friendly roles
Target companies that:
- sponsor visas from India, or
- hire remote now and allow relocation later, or
- have offices in Europe / Canada / NZ / Australia and are open to later transfer

### Track C: Bridge roles
Target roles that strengthen the profile while keeping future relocation possible:
- senior/staff full-stack
- AI platform / product engineering
- frontend-heavy TS/React
- distributed systems / developer tools

## Decision Rules
### Apply immediately if:
- remote is clearly global or India-friendly
- role is senior/staff and matches TS/React/Node/AI
- company is in Europe or relocation-friendly

### Skip or deprioritize if:
- remote is US-only and no India overlap
- role is too junior
- stack is far from React/TypeScript/backend/AI
- sponsorship is unclear and not worth the effort
